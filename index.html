Interpretation & data quality

Research strengths
Rapid output growth; overall FWCI above 1.00; substantial top-decile representation; extensive international collaboration.
Important limitations
Faculty attribution is provisional. 168 of 253 publications were candidate-matched; 85 lacked reliable roster matches. Historical staff records, verified Scopus Author IDs and official KPI targets are required for reliable college/department analysis.

ASJC shares, collaboration categories, citation percentiles and source quartiles are published aggregates. They are not available as verified publication-level joins in the supplied model; accordingly, changing the year or subject filter does not invent cross-filtered values.
